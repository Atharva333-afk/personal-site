Privion.

A hackathon project about privacy around listening devices, built with Siddhant Samar and R Jaiswal.

NTHS Hack 2026Team projectGeneral 1st Place

What we tried

Our Devpost submission describes an anti-eavesdropping concept and a prototype using HTML, Python, sensors, and hardware design. One problem we ran into was sound: making the voice hard to understand without making the cancellation noise too loud.

The project page records what the team built, what did not work cleanly, and the next hardware steps we had in mind. It was submitted to NTHS Hack 2026 and won the General 1st Place prize.
